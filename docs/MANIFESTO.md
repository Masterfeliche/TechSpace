# The TechSpace Manifesto

**TechSpace is where DIT students become people who ship products that real people use.**

Silicon Valley is not a place. It is a loop: people build things, some of them work, and
the people who succeed come back to teach, hire and back the next ones. Every turn of
that loop makes the next one faster.

We are building that loop at the Dar es Salaam Institute of Technology. Not by copying an
address, but by copying the habits: build in public, ship to real users, teach what you
learn, and lift the next builder up.

## What we believe

1. **Shipped beats perfect.** A live product with fifty users outranks a polished
   repository nobody ran. We measure work by what reaches people, not by what sits on a
   laptop.
2. **Real users, real problems.** We build for DIT and Dar es Salaam first: timetables,
   parking, attendance, payments, the things we live next to every day. Problems we can
   touch are problems we can solve.
3. **Learn in public.** Code, demos, write-ups and post-mortems are shared by default.
   Hoarded knowledge dies with the person who holds it; shared knowledge compounds.
4. **Pay it forward.** Every builder who has shipped teaches one who hasn't yet.
   Mentorship is not a favour here. It is the price of membership, and the engine of
   everything else.
5. **Earn your seat by contribution.** Status comes from what you shipped and who you
   helped, not from your year of study or your title. Anyone can rise; nobody coasts.
6. **Fail fast, fail safely, say so.** Most products will not work. That is the point of
   trying. We end experiments honestly, write down why, and make the next attempt better.

## The builder loop

**Learn → Build → Ship → Teach → Lift.** Every activity at TechSpace should move someone
one step along this loop. If it doesn't, we stop doing it.

## How we are organised

| Layer | Who | What happens | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Learn** | Every DIT student | Weekly sessions along an engineering roadmap; every session ends with something built | Junior builders with real fundamentals |
| **Build** | By application | Product teams of 4–6 with a mentor, a public repo, a live URL and one metric | Contributors with shipped work |
| **Launch** | Products with traction | Pilots with DIT departments or local businesses; introductions to alumni and funders | Products and founders we stand behind |

## Our rhythm

- **Weekly:** Demo Day (every team shows something working), Build Night, Teach-back.
- **Quarterly:** Hackathon, Open House, Post-mortem Night.
- **Yearly:** Showcase, and a public *State of TechSpace* report.

## What every member commits to

- Show something working at demo day, every week you are on a team.
- Keep your code public unless there is a real reason not to.
- Mentor at least one newer member each semester once you have shipped.
- Tell the truth about what failed, in demos, post-mortems and reviews.
- Leave TechSpace stronger than you found it, and come back when you can.

## What we measure

Not followers or attendance. We count products live and their weekly users, features
shipped, members who shipped something in the last 90 days, mentoring hours, and alumni
placed in jobs or building companies.
